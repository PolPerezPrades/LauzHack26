## 5. Decisions

Format: `D-A3NN · YYYY-MM-DD · decision` — *why* — status.

- **D-A301 · 2026-11-22 · The reset is for the MX Brio only:** a label containing "MX Brio" (`isMxBrio`). The desktop preference keeps matching any Brio (`/brio/i`, Part A), so another Brio model is still preferred but never sent PTZ. — *The user asked for the MX Brio and "every other camera untouched"; its PTZ ranges are measured; no other Brio is in the project.* — active
- **D-A302 · 2026-11-22 · When to ask for PTZ access.**
  - A request names pan, tilt and zoom (`true`, only the ones `getSupportedConstraints()` lists) only when the camera is known to be an MX Brio. That means its label seen before opening (saved choice, switcher), the label found after the default camera opened (Brio preference), or a Brio first opened plain because labels were hidden before the first grant: that Brio is opened once more, asking.
  - The plain stream stays open until the PTZ one is, so a refused or failed PTZ request never costs the camera, and a PTZ refusal never turns into the `denied` camera state.
  - *Why, measured in Chrome 157 on the Brio:* PTZ capabilities and settings appear only once the origin has the PTZ permission, whether or not the request asked. Chrome only prompts for that permission when a request names pan, tilt or zoom. Labels are hidden before the first camera grant. — active
- **D-A303 · 2026-11-22 · The helper also takes the current settings:** `brioReset(label, caps, settings?)` returns only the controls that are out of place, and null when the Brio is already at its defaults. Targets: zoom `caps.zoom.min`, pan and tilt 0 clamped into their range, only for the controls the browser exposes. It refines the part's "(label, capabilities)", which is still the call without `settings`. — *A reset that changes nothing still costs about 75 ms (measured).* — active
- **D-A304 · 2026-11-22 · The reset runs before the stream is handed out,** and keeps the track's other constraints (`{ ...getConstraints() without pan/tilt/zoom, advanced: [reset] }`). It waits at most `RESET_WAIT_MS` = 1500 ms (`lib/camera.ts`) and swallows a refusal. — *So the first frame on screen is already at full field of view. The reset took 74–611 ms in Chrome; the cap means a slow device never blocks the camera. 1280×720@30 stays (checked).* — active
- **D-A305 · 2026-11-22 · Every path that opens a camera goes through one function** (`openCamera` → `withBrioDefaults`): saved choice, phone front camera and its fallback, Brio preference, a default camera that is the Brio, and `switchCamera` (the in-card switcher). The camera choice order is unchanged (saved → phone front camera → Brio → default). — *The Brio can arrive through any of them, not only the two the checklist names.* — active

## 6. Open questions

Format: `Q-A3NN` question — *Default: what you assumed meanwhile.*

- **Q-A301** The Python prototype also leaves the Brio's exposure and focus in manual mode (V4L2: auto exposure off, exposure time 60, gain 255, manual focus at 40, backlight compensation off). Chrome reports `exposureMode: "manual"` and `focusMode: "manual"` for it, so the preview can look dark, noisy or soft. Chrome would accept `exposureMode`/`focusMode: "continuous"` in the same `applyConstraints` call, without the PTZ permission. Should the app reset those too? — *Default: no. The spec and your request cover zoom, pan and tilt only, and `view_cam.sh` doesn't reset them either.*

## 7. Risks, known issues and stubs

Format: `R-A3NN` description.

- **R-A301** In a new Chrome profile, the first MX Brio card adds Chrome's second prompt, "use and move your camera" (PTZ, R-024). Blocked or dismissed, the Brio still opens, unreset. While it stays blocked, Chrome doesn't ask again: allow it again from the site's permission settings (the icon left of the address).
- **R-A302** The real PTZ prompt was not tested by a machine. Chrome's `--use-fake-ui-for-media-stream` grants the camera but never PTZ (measured), so the checks emulate the prompt: the instrumented `getUserMedia` pauses, and the script answers with `Browser.setPermission`. The prompt's wording and the click are part of the user's look (§10).
- **R-A303** Firefox and Safari expose no PTZ for webcams (Firefox 158: no pan, tilt or zoom in `getSupportedConstraints()`), so there the Brio stays as the device has it, without an error. Manual reset: `./view_cam.sh`, or `python3 camctl.py /dev/v4l/by-id/usb-046d_MX_Brio_*-video-index0 zoom_absolute=100 pan_absolute=0 tilt_absolute=0`. The field-of-view setting (65°/78°/90°) is not reachable from a page (R-024).
- **R-A304** When labels are hidden before the first grant (first visit, or a one-time "allow this time" grant), a Brio that is the saved or default camera opens twice on the page's first camera card (plain, then asking for PTZ). That costs about one extra second; later cards, and later visits with a lasting grant, open it once.
