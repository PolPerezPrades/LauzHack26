## 2. Checklist

Tick an item only when it is implemented **and** verified.

**First increment (by 10:45)**

- [ ] No viewer chrome on feed cards: no Replay, Slow or reset-view chips, no "3D hand / Avatar / Real video" label, no credit line ("3D hand · pose checked against…"). The sign loops on its own. Use `SignViewer`'s existing `controls={false}` and `credit={false}` (and `loop`) in every task view; `#lab` keeps its controls (D-053).
- [ ] Lesson: the watch step goes to the try step on its own after the sign has played (about two loops, a constant in `learningConfig.ts`), with no "Now you" button. ↑, Enter and Space still skip ahead.
- [ ] Camera try (letters with detector rules): the camera starts by itself and the hold tracker passes the card as soon as the hand matches. No "skip", "I'm stuck" or record buttons in the feed.
- [ ] Time limit: if the hand hasn't matched within `tooLateMs` (about 8 s, in `learningConfig.ts`, with a visible countdown ring or bar), the card ends as wrong with the result "Too late!" ("¡Demasiado tarde!"), showing the answer. A2's banner then moves on by itself.
- [ ] Nothing in the feed asks for the mirror or a self-grade: an item with no detector rule (words, phrases, J, Z, numbers) is never asked productively. Its lesson is watch-only (it plays, then the card leaves as "seen"), and the generators of `question` and `lesson`'s try step decline it, so the card source picks something else (D-052). Mirror and self-check stay in the code for `#dev`.
- [ ] Camera off or denied: receptive formats and watch-only lessons only; no camera prompt inside a card.
- [ ] Gates green; the feed checked in the browser (desktop webcam): a letter lesson passes hands-free, and a wrong hand ends with "Too late!".
